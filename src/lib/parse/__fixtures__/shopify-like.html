Hand-thrown stoneware vase with a matte glaze, 24 cm tall. Each piece is shaped on the wheel in our studio, dried slowly for a week and fired twice. Because every vase is made by hand, small variations in the glaze and the rim are expected — they are the point, not a defect.

  • Watertight, hand wash only
  • Made in Tbilisi

Shipping and returns

Orders leave the studio within two working days. Domestic delivery takes two to four days; international shipping is calculated at checkout and usually arrives within two weeks. Every parcel is packed in recycled cardboard with paper padding, and we reuse boxes wherever they are still in good shape.

If a piece arrives damaged, send us a photo within fourteen days and we will replace it or refund the order in full. Unused items can be returned within thirty days; return shipping is on us for domestic orders and shared for international ones.

Care

Hand wash with warm water and a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive sponges and dishwasher cycles: the matte glaze keeps its texture far longer when it is not blasted with detergent every week. The vase is watertight and can hold fresh flowers; empty it fully between arrangements so mineral rings do not settle on the inside wall.
